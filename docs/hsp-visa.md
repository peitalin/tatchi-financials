## 2) Why age was used with salary points

In the current HSP ministerial ordinance, the salary item for (i)(a)/(i)(b) is age-conditioned:

- under 30: income bands `イ〜ト`
- 30 to under 35: `イ〜ヘ`
- 35 to under 40: `イ〜ホ`
- 40+: `イ〜ハ`

For age 36 (your case), only up to `ホ` is available. That means:

- JPY 4M to under 5M (`ト`) is not usable at age 36
- JPY 5M to under 6M (`ヘ`) is not usable at age 36
- JPY 6M to under 7M (`ホ`) is the first salary band that scores for age 36

## 7) 準ずる実績 (equivalent achievements): realistic reading

You are correct that `準ずる実績` exists and is real. The ordinance includes “other research achievements equivalent to listed achievements” recognized by the Minister after consultation.

Important nuance:

- It is **possible**, not automatic.
- Decision quality depends heavily on evidence package quality and legal framing.

Your audited security-critical OSS can be used, especially if evidence shows:

- objective technical significance
- real-world adoption / production relevance
- independent third-party validation (audits, external reviews)
- direct relevance to the intended work in Japan
