## Required documents checklist
- [ ] 01 Form 1-1: Startup Preparation Activity Plan Confirmation Application and Consent
  - Direct link: https://www.investtokyo.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en/oursupports/bdc-tokyo/form_1-1.docx
- [ ] 02 Form 1-3: Startup Preparation Activity Plan
  - Direct link: https://www.investtokyo.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/jp/oursupports/bdc-tokyo/Form%201-3.docx
- [ ] 03 Form 1-4: Resume
  - Direct link: https://www.investtokyo.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/jp/oursupports/bdc-tokyo/Form%201-4.docx
- [ ] 04 Copy of passport
- [ ] 05 Proof of residence plan for 1 year after entry/status change
- [ ] 06 Proof of living/accommodation funds for 1 year
- [ ] 07 Qualification proof under revised criteria (at least one)
  - 1+ year business management experience, or
  - Doctoral/Master/professional degree in relevant field
- [ ] 08 Other supporting documents (recommended)
  - Examples: language certificate, product materials, customer intent, achievements
- [ ] 09 Power of attorney (only if proxy applies)
- [ ] 10 Proxy credential proof (only if proxy applies)

## How to obtain (non-download documents)
- Passport copy: scan passport bio page in color and ensure all text is legible.
- Residence proof: prepare lease application/contract or equivalent long-term accommodation evidence.
- Living funds proof: request a bank balance statement showing sufficient funds for the period.
- Qualification proof: collect employment certificates and/or degree certificates matching the selected path.
- Proxy docs: if using a proxy, include a power of attorney and proxy qualification/registration evidence.

## Submission note
- Tokyo currently accepts application data by email to `support2@bdc-tokyo.org` (verify latest instructions on the official page before sending).
